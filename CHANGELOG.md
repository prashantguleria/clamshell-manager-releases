## 2.0.0 - 2026-10-21

First public release.

### Added

- One switch in the menu bar keeps the Mac awake with the lid closed, with no password prompt.
- Rules that turn keep-awake off: low battery, charger unplugged, no external display, running hot, and a timer.
- Live CPU temperature readout in °C.
- Turns back on when the charger is plugged in again or once the Mac has cooled down.
- Scheduled wake after a heat stop with the lid closed.
- Optional background helper, approved once in Login Items, for lid-closed use on battery.
- Signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple.
